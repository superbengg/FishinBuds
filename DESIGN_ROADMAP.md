# A little company by the water.

Fishin' Buds makes fishing in Azeroth feel social—even when you're fishing alone.
Its identity is **fishing utility + an in-game social fishing feed presented
through an Azeroth-style Gnomish/Goblin communicator**. Statistics support that
experience; they must not gradually become the whole product or a tournament app.

## Five tabs, one fishing companion

- **Feed:** what is happening.
- **Me:** my fishing life.
- **Buds:** my fishing friends/community.
- **Realm:** broader fishing activity.
- **Records:** fishing history and records.

The Feed will become asynchronous: Trophy catches, personal bests, Bud records,
rare and exceptional catches, and Trophy screenshots. A small fixed set of
reactions is welcome. **No in-game Feed comments.** Conversation belongs in the
Fishin' Buds Discord/community rather than beneath every catch. Current posts
are local only; never fabricate social activity.

**NOW THAT is a nice fish.** belongs to exceptional catches. Keep it uncommon.

The current Gnomish Field Communicator is an Azeroth-built communication device,
with a possible Goblin alternate skin later. It naturally appears when fishing
begins, providing useful readings and something interesting between casts.
It must never obstruct bobber clicks, looting, movement, or combat.

## Lightweight roadmap — not implemented in this maintenance pass

- Test Retail high-score/Trophy handling with real catches.
- Refine Records cards and richer catch presentation/icons.
- Prototype the Buds social experience.
- Investigate Friends/Guild integration.
- Investigate external synchronization and a companion.
- Investigate automatic Trophy screenshots.
- Investigate website profiles and leaderboards.
- Explore Discord community integration.
- Add a Goblin communicator skin.
- Explore a WoW Forever / Classic provider and weighted-fish records where supported.
- Evolve the cross-version Catch Record abstraction around actual provider evidence.

No schedule or backend is promised. Preserve the working fishing loop and honest
data boundaries while exploring these ideas. Fishing utility first, personality
second, social platform later—with the social destination always in view.
